Everything between the purchase ticket and the sale check

🐂 Lots, not spreadsheets

Log head, in-weight and price by the cwt or by the head. HerdLedger estimates today's weight from your gain and tracks death loss and closed-out lots.

🧾 Every cost, per head & per cwt

Feed, yardage, vet, freight, interest and commission roll into a cost basis and cost of gain. Project what it'll take to reach your target weight or sale date.

📈 Breakeven vs. the board

Each lot is valued at the futures close plus your local basis, or your own cash bid, so you can see profit or loss pen by pen.

🛡️ Hedges in the same picture

Futures, puts and calls, and LRP endorsements are marked to the latest settlement, with a running tally of how much of your cattle is protected.

📊 Equity you can show the bank

A snapshot is saved every day you open the app, and you can record one any time. Watch the trend and print a clean summary for your lender.

📤 Your data, your way

Printable lot and operation reports, CSV export for your accountant, and a one-click backup file.

Market prices are end-of-day / delayed settlements for live cattle, feeder cattle and corn, updated each weekday evening. HerdLedger does not show real-time quotes.
